# PhotoLab Privacy Policy

PhotoLab is designed as a local-first photo editor.

## Photos

PhotoLab does not upload your photos to a server for editing, enhancement, background replacement, export, or storage. Browser edits run in your browser. Mobile edits run on your device.

## Accounts

PhotoLab does not require an account.

## Local Storage

On web, PhotoLab stores gallery metadata and edit recipes in browser storage so you can keep working locally. Clearing site data may remove locally saved projects.

On iOS and Android, PhotoLab stores imported photos, generated previews, and recipes in the app sandbox.

## Analytics

This app currently does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.
